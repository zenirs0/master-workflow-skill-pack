# Design brief

- **Audience and task** — [Who uses this and what should they finish?]
- **Screen flow** — [Entry, decisions, primary action, completion, recovery]
- **Visual direction** — [References, assets, typography, palette, and spacing]
- **Components and states** — [Default, focus, loading, empty, error, success]
- **Mobile behavior** — [Layout changes, long text, touch and keyboard controls]
- **Accessibility** — [Labels, keyboard order, focus, contrast, reduced motion]
- **Handoff** — [Figma link or implementation, asset paths, verification evidence]
